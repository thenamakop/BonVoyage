## Method

Same conventions as batch C1 (`docs/sprints/catalogue-c1-pois-2026-10-26.md`, "Method"): location from the Google listing to 4 decimals and linked by its `cid`; hours from the listing as first opening to last closing, with Closed days as `closed_days`; open-air places with no gate and no listed hours, and "Open 24 hours", leave both times empty; fees are `free` for public places with no ticket, otherwise an exact figure from an official, operator or current secondary page, or `draft`; `typical_minutes` by category convention. Two points specific to this batch:

- **Where trawell.in and the Google listing disagree on hours or the closed day** (Jim Corbett's Dhangarhi museum aside, this happens at the Darwan Singh museum, St Mary's Church and the zoo), the file follows the Google listing, which is the more current of the two, and the row says so. The fee still comes from trawell.in when it is the only exact figure.
- **Kanatal shares four places with Dhanaulti** (Surkanda Devi, Kaudia forest, Kaddukhal viewpoint, Dhanaulti Eco Park): the two towns are 10 km apart and visitors to either go to the same places. The Kanatal rows repeat the Dhanaulti rows' values under their own `poi_key`.

**Candidates left out:**
- `haridwar/shantikunj`: no page states visiting hours (trawell.in lists none).
- `lansdowne/kaleshwar-mahadev-temple`: no hours on Google or trawell.in.
- `lansdowne/santoshi-mata-temple`: no hours on Google or trawell.in.
- `jim-corbett/jim-corbett-museum`: 48 km from the catalogue point (at Kaladhungi), well past the 25 km limit.
- `mukteshwar/starscapes-observatory`: priced per session; no entry fee.
