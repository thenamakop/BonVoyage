## Method

### Stays

- **Source:** Booking.com search results for one room, 2 adults, one night. The check-in is Saturday 21 November 2026, and prices are in INR including the "taxes and charges" line.
- **Pushkar exception:** Pushkar uses Saturday 5 December, because 21 November falls in the Pushkar Fair week (Kartik Purnima is 24 November). The fair-week read gave ₹1,800 / ₹4,200 / ₹14,200, about double.
- **Left out:**
  - dormitory beds;
  - whole villas, houses or apartments with 2 or more bedrooms;
  - properties more than 20 km from Booking's centre for the search (40 km where fewer than 5 rooms remained);
  - the extra properties Booking lists after its "N properties found" count, which belong to neighbouring towns.
- **Budget:** the median of the 5 cheapest remaining rooms rated "Good: 7+", in the list sorted "Price (lowest first)". Neemrana has only 4 rated properties, so its 5 cheapest of all 8 were used.
- **Mid and premium:** the median and the 80th percentile (linear interpolation) of the first 50 results in Booking's default order ("Our top picks"), or of all results where the town has fewer.
- **Rounding:** all three tiers to the nearest ₹100.
- **Search terms:** "Name, State, India", with three exceptions:
  - Bhimtal: "Bhim Tal, Uttarakhand, India", because "Bhimtal" resolves to the Bhimtal Lake landmark, which spans Nainital district;
  - Jim Corbett: "Jim Corbett National Park", the landmark, because "Corbett, Uttarakhand" returns 1 property;
  - Ranthambore: "Sawai Madhopur, Rajasthan, India", the town where the park's hotels are, because "Ranthambore" resolves to a single resort.

### Food

- **Source:** a Google Maps search for "restaurants" centred on the destination's catalogue coordinates at zoom 14, in English for India.
  - Jim Corbett is centred on Ramnagar and Dhikuli (29.43, 79.13), because the catalogue point is inside the park.
  - Ranthambore is centred on Ranthambore Road (26.01, 76.38).
- **What was read:** the first 40 to 60 results, and each restaurant's "price per person" range as reported by Google users. Between 13 and 46 restaurants per town show a range.
- **Typical meal:** the median of the range midpoints.
  - A range wider than ₹400 counts as its lowest ₹400, so ₹400 to ₹1,400 counts as ₹600. This stops a few bars and fine-dining rooms with wide ranges from setting the figure.
  - "₹2,000+" counts as ₹2,000, and a lower bound of ₹1 is read as ₹0.
- **Food per day:** 2.5 typical meals (lunch, dinner and a breakfast at half price), to the nearest ₹50.
- **Why not "restaurants in Town":** that query ranks a city's best-known restaurants first. It gave Dehradun ₹800 a meal (₹2,000 a day), and Jaipur ₹700.

### Local

- **Source:** CabBazar, a taxi aggregator that publishes a full-day local hire rate (8 hours, 80 km) for each town. It uses the town's "Local Sightseeing" page where there is one, and otherwise its city page ("Full day taxi price").
- **Local per day:** the AC hatchback full-day rate ÷ `persons_per_car` (4), to the nearest ₹50. So each trip day carries one day of local hire shared by four, which covers local transport and sundries.
- **Towns without a CabBazar page:**
  - Dhanaulti uses Mussoorie (25 km);
  - Kanatal uses Chamba, Tehri (12 km);
  - Sariska uses Alwar (35 km).
- **Almora:** CabBazar's Almora page shows ₹4,800, twice Nainital (₹2,400) and more than three times Ranikhet and Mukteshwar (₹1,400). This is treated as an error on that page, and Nainital's rate is used. Maulik to confirm.

### Car rental (cost rule)

- **Source:** Zoomcar Delhi, self-drive, 24 hours from 09:00 Saturday 21 November to 09:00 Sunday 22 November 2026, with the "Hatchback" filter and the default 300 km plan.
- **What was read:** 90 hatchbacks were listed with a price, mainly Baleno (37), Swift (10), Ignis (8), Altroz (7) and Glanza (6).
- **Result:** the median "excluding fees" price is ₹2,376, which rounds to **₹2,400**.
  - A Tuesday-to-Wednesday read on the same day gave ₹2,328.
  - Zoomcar's convenience fee and trip protection are added at checkout, and are not shown without signing in. The refundable deposit is not a cost. Neither is included.
- **Cross-check:** the first pass's 2026 guide range, ₹1,500 to ₹2,500 a day, agrees.

### Limits

- **Booking.com coverage:** Booking lists only properties that are bookable online. Very cheap local lodges are missing, so budget stays read high in small towns: Chakrata ₹2,500, Kanatal ₹2,500 and Sariska ₹4,200.
- **One reference night:** weekday and off-season prices are lower, and festival weeks are higher.
- **Sariska:** almost every listing is a safari lodge, giving ₹4,200 / ₹13,600 / ₹17,600. A budget group would stay in Alwar.
- **Food ranges:** Google's ranges are user-reported and coarse, so most towns come to ₹750 a day.
- **Local rates:** CabBazar's rates are its published "starts from" fares, and many small towns show the same ₹1,400.
