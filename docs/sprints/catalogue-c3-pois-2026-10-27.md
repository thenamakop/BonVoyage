## Method

Same conventions as batch C1 (`docs/sprints/catalogue-c1-pois-2026-10-26.md`, "Method"), with one stricter rule for fees:

- **trawell.in alone is not accepted for a Rajasthan fee.** Batches C1 and C2 accepted trawell.in when it was the only page with an exact fee. For Rajasthan it is demonstrably out of date: Mehrangarh Rs 70 on trawell.in against Rs 200 on mehrangarh.org; Jaipur state monuments Rs 50 against Rs 100 since the August 2025 revision; Alwar Museum Rs 5 against Rs 20 on the Museums of India portal; Saheliyon Ki Bari Rs 5. So a Rajasthan fee is verified only from the operator's own page (mehrangarh.org) or the Ministry of Culture's Museums of India portal where its hours also agree with the Google listing, or two current pages that agree. trawell.in is still accepted for "Entry: Free" at temples and gardens.
- **Sources that could not be read:** the Rajasthan monuments booking site (obms-tourist.rajasthan.gov.in) refuses connections from both browsers; the ASI ticketed-monuments page blocks automated reading; the City Palace Udaipur ticket page needs location access; Google search showed a robot check, which was not bypassed.
- **Hours where trawell.in or an official page disagrees with the Google listing:** the file follows Google, as in C2, and the row says so.
- **Distance:** three places sit 25 to 33 km from the catalogue point and are kept because they are why people visit (Deeg Palace from Bharatpur, Karni Mata Temple at Deshnok from Bikaner, Bhangarh from Sariska).

**Candidates left out:**
- `ranthambore/jogi-mahal`: inside the park and not open to visitors on its own; no hours.
- `ranthambore/amreshwar-mahadev-temple`: no hours on Google or trawell.in.
- `udaipur/lake-pichola-boat-ride`: a boat ride rather than a place; its price is not on a current page.
