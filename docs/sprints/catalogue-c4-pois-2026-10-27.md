## Method

Same conventions as batch C1 (`docs/sprints/catalogue-c1-pois-2026-10-26.md`, "Method"), with the batch C3 fee rule extended to Himachal Pradesh: trawell.in is out of date here too (Viceregal Lodge Rs 20 on trawell.in against Rs 100 on iias.org), so a fee is verified only from the operator's own page or two current pages that agree. trawell.in is still accepted for "Entry: Free" and for hours where the Google listing has none. Where the official page and the Google listing disagree on hours, the file follows Google, as in C2 and C3.

Five rows are draft for hours, not fees: Chokling Monastery, Kali Ka Tibba, Sidh Baba Ka Mandir, St John's Church Dalhousie and St Mary's Church Kotgarh have no hours on their Google listings or any readable page, and are not open-air places.

**Candidates left out:**
- `chail/chail-palace`: a hotel; day visits are not sold as a fixed entry.
- `narkanda/oaktree-waterfall`: the Google listing's hours are inconsistent (Saturday 7 pm to midnight) and the place has few reviews.
