## Method

Same conventions as batch C1 (`docs/sprints/catalogue-c1-pois-2026-10-26.md`, "Method") and the C3 fee rule: a fee is verified only from the operator's own page or two current pages that agree. Points specific to this batch:

- **Agra fees** come from the official Taj Mahal site's monument fee table (Agra Development Authority and ASI), which covers all five ticketed Agra rows.
- **Sunrise-to-sunset monuments:** the Taj Mahal opens "30 Minutes before sunrise" and closes "30 Minutes before sunset", and Agra Fort has no hours on Google or the official site. A fixed HH:MM is required, so these rows use a **sunrise convention of 06:00 to 18:00**. Itimad-ud-Daulah also uses it, because its Google listing shows 8 am to midnight, which is not plausible for an ASI monument. See Maulik to check.
- **Holiday entries:** Google showed a one-off holiday line for 30 or 31 October on many listings ("National Unity Day", "Haryana Foundation Day"); those lines are ignored and the regular day is used.
- **Hours inside a fort:** Teli Ka Mandir and the Sas-Bahu temples have no hours of their own and take Gwalior Fort's listing hours.
- **ASI fees outside Agra:** the ASI fee page cannot be read automatically and the newest readable notice is from 2016, so ASI-ticketed rows outside Agra (Gwalior, the Residency) are draft.
